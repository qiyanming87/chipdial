ChipDial Support

ChipDial is an offline machining calculator for machinists, CNC operators, and small shops.

Getting help

Use the in-app calculator that matches your operation, choose Imperial or Metric units, enter the measured tool and process values, and review the result before use.

Questions or feedback

For support, bug reports, or suggestions, email levinghost@outlook.com. Please include your device model, iOS version, app version, and the steps that reproduce the issue. Do not send passwords, payment details, or other sensitive information.

Safety

ChipDial provides starting-point calculations. Always verify tooling data, workholding, machine limits, material condition, and safe operating procedures before cutting.
