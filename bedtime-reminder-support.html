中文常见问题

如何开始免费提醒？

从主页设置提醒时间并开启每日提醒。无需开始试用或订阅。现版本开启每日提醒计划需要屏幕使用时间授权；可在设置相应区域点“继续”，由系统向你请求访问。通知需单独允许，拒绝通知不影响已获屏幕使用时间许可的遮罩。日历、手动记录和皮肤不要求这两项权限。

屏幕使用时间会读取应用内容吗？

App 使用系统提供的不透明应用、类别及网站标识来显示遮罩，不读取所选应用内容或全设备最后操作时间。可使用所有类别范围模式或自行选择；系统选择器由你操作，App 不伪造全选。可在 App 关闭提醒，或在系统设置撤销屏幕使用时间访问。

锁屏时如何提醒？

允许通知后，系统可在锁屏、顶部横幅和通知中心显示本地提醒；不会强行自动打开 App 或在桌面/锁屏覆盖遮罩。点击通知并解锁后进入提醒页。若没有提示，请检查通知权限、显示位置、专注模式和通知摘要，并确认提醒已开启。无需核对试用或订阅资格；现版本每日提醒计划仍需屏幕使用时间授权。

如何延后或记录就寝？

选择“10 分钟后再提醒”可安排延后，并清除同晚已记录的准备就寝时间，重新等待确认。“我准备睡觉了”记录确认时刻并解除本次提醒；未回应保持未记录。主页可取消延后，关闭提醒会停止后续排程和本 App 的遮罩。

就寝日历的日期如何归属？

日历来自你确认或手动录入的时间，不是睡眠检测。记录时间不晚于目标为达标，其后为晚睡。凌晨 00:00–05:59 的目标归前一晚，例如 14 日 00:30 属于 13 日晚；前一晚记录可选次日凌晨的有效时间。未来日期不可新增。修改提醒时间会清除受影响的当前晚记录，已结束的历史夜晚保留。

月订阅购买什么？是否必须订阅？

提醒、屏幕使用时间遮罩、日历和小组件免费。经典紫免费，其他四款皮肤由可选 Apple 月订阅解锁。价格和计费周期以 App 与 Apple 确认页显示为准；只有你确认购买才会订阅，按月自动续费，除非取消。旧版本试用已不限制提醒，不会自动转为订阅。皮肤不要求屏幕使用时间或通知授权。

在哪里恢复或取消购买？到期会怎样？

从设置进入皮肤月订阅，使用购买时的 Apple 账户恢复购买，或打开“管理或取消订阅”。取消后，在 Apple 返回的有效期限内保留皮肤权益。到期或退款后恢复经典紫；免费提醒和日历不受订阅变化影响。删除 App 不会取消订阅，Apple 独立保留其交易记录。

如何撤销权限或删除数据？

可在系统“设置 → 屏幕使用时间 → 有屏幕使用时间访问权限的 App”关闭就寝提醒；App 再次获知撤权时会暂停计划并取消其排程和通知。通知可在“设置 → 通知 → 就寝提醒”单独关闭，名称随系统版本变化。日历记录可逐条删除；删除 App 可移除普通本地数据，但“卸载 App”可能保留文稿，旧试用钥匙串、系统备份和 Apple 交易不保证随之删除。当前没有一键清除全部数据入口，详见完整隐私政策。

如何设置语言、小组件或联系开发者？

设置支持中文、英文和跟随系统；其他系统语言默认英文。小组件从系统桌面编辑界面添加，刷新由系统安排。在设置中可打开完整隐私政策及公开网页。需要帮助请联系 levinghost@outlook.com；可自愿提供重现步骤、设备与 App 版本，不要发送 Apple 密码或支付资料。邮件由你确认后发送，App 不自动附加诊断。

English FAQ

How do I start free reminders?

Set a time and enable daily reminders on the home screen. No trial or subscription is required. This version requires Screen Time authorization for the daily reminder plan. Tap Continue in the relevant Settings section for the system access request. Notifications require separate permission; declining notifications does not prevent already-authorized Screen Time shields. The calendar, manual entries and skins do not require either permission.

Does Screen Time read app content?

The app uses opaque system app, category and website tokens to apply shields, without reading selected-app content or a device-wide last interaction time. Use the all-categories scope mode or choose your own scope. You operate the system picker; the app does not fabricate an all-selected picker. Turn reminders off in the app or revoke Screen Time access in system settings.

How do reminders work while locked?

With notification permission, the system can show local alerts on the Lock Screen, in banners and in Notification Center. The app does not force itself open or cover the Home or Lock Screen with a shield. Tap a notification and unlock to open the reminder page. If alerts are missing, check permission, presentation settings, Focus and Scheduled Summary, and ensure reminders are on. Trial or subscription eligibility is not required. This version still needs Screen Time authorization for its daily reminder plan.

How do I snooze or record bedtime?

Remind me in 10 minutes schedules a snooze and clears any confirmed entry for the same night, waiting for a new confirmation. I’m ready for bed records your confirmation time and dismisses this reminder. No response leaves the night unrecorded. You can cancel a snooze on the home screen; turning reminders off stops later schedules and this app’s shields.

How are calendar nights assigned?

Entries come from your confirmation or manual input, not sleep detection. A reported bedtime at or before its goal is on time; a later one is late. Goals from 00:00 through 05:59 belong to the previous night: 00:30 on the 14th belongs to the night of the 13th. An entry for the preceding night can use an eligible early-morning time on the following date. Future dates cannot be added. Changing the reminder time clears affected current-night entries; completed past nights are kept.

What does the monthly subscription unlock? Is it required?

Reminders, Screen Time shields, the calendar and widgets are free. Classic Purple is free; an optional Apple monthly subscription unlocks the other four skins. Price and billing period are shown in the app and Apple confirmation sheet. A subscription begins only after your confirmation and renews monthly unless cancelled. A legacy trial no longer limits reminders and never automatically becomes a subscription. Skins require neither Screen Time nor notification permission.

Where do I restore or cancel? What happens on expiry?

Open the skin subscription page in Settings. Restore purchases using the purchasing Apple Account, or open Manage or cancel subscription. After cancellation, skin access remains for the valid period reported by Apple. Expiration or refund returns the appearance to Classic Purple; free reminders and the calendar are unaffected. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription. Apple retains its transaction records separately.

How do I revoke permissions or delete data?

Turn off Bedtime Reminder in system Settings → Screen Time → Apps with Screen Time Access. When the app next observes revocation, it pauses the plan and cancels its schedules and notifications. Separately turn off notifications under Settings → Notifications → Bedtime Reminder; names vary by system version. Calendar entries can be deleted individually. Delete App removes ordinary local data, while Offload App may retain documents. Legacy trial Keychain items, system backups and Apple transactions are not guaranteed to be removed. There is no one-tap erase-all control. See the full privacy policy.
