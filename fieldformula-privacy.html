FieldFormula Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 11, 2026

FieldFormula is an offline reference calculator for building, renovation, woodworking, electrical, HVAC, and plumbing estimates. The app does not require an account and does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data.

Data stored on your device

Projects, calculation history, and preferences may be stored locally on your device. This information is not sent to the developer or any third party.

Tracking and third parties

FieldFormula does not use advertising, analytics, tracking technologies, or third-party data collection services.

Changes and contact

For privacy questions, email levinghost@outlook.com.
