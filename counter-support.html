帮助与支持

联系 QYM

需要帮助或提出隐私请求，请发送邮件至 levinghost@outlook.com。提供 App版本、系统版本、设备型号、问题步骤和大致发生时间即可。截图请遮盖私人名称与数值；不要提供密码、完整设备标识、IDFA、广告密钥或敏感支付资料。点击邮箱只打开邮件应用，由你决定是否发送。

支持资料保存

支持问题结案后90天删除相关邮件及附件，法律要求保留的资料除外。此规则仅适用于开发者管理的副本；邮件服务商和备份按各自安排处理，不适用于 Unity、Apple 或其他第三方持有的资料。

新增、修改与归零

新增会直接创建计数器，之后可在该计数器设置中修改名称、颜色、运算、起始值和目标等选项。卡片或详情中的按钮用于计数。单击归零会要求确认，双击可直接归零；归零使用该计数器的起始值。全部归零、删除卡片和清除历史请留意对应提示。归零不等于清除历史，也不会删除计数器。

本地数据与分享

计数及设置保存在当前设备，没有独立用户账号或自建云同步。换设备、卸载或重装前，请保留需要的内容，不能假设自动恢复。分享由你主动发送；单卡图片不是包含全部历史与设置的完整备份。系统备份和接收方保存的副本由各自服务管理。

提醒、计划与小组件

提醒需要系统通知许可，拒绝后仍可计数；可在系统设置检查锁屏显示。计划功能仅按当前版本说明的条件执行，不保证应用关闭后持续运行。提供小组件时，它读取本地共享快照，系统更新可能延迟，且无需广告或跟踪许可。遇到未更新，请先在 App 中确认数值和保存结果，再反馈步骤。

广告隐私选择

当前广告未启用，后续仅经授权测试。你可以在设置的广告隐私入口拒绝或撤回，继续免费基本计数。撤回停止 App 后续广告请求与刷新、销毁当前横幅；厂商已收数据需另行申请处理。ATT 与广告同意是分别管理的选择，系统跟踪设置不会代替广告同意。详见隐私政策、Unity 隐私政策及Apple 跟踪权限说明。

高级版购买与恢复

正式购买尚未开放，没有高级版账户登录。将来开放的非消耗型高级版由 Apple 原生购买与恢复确认权益，测试展示不代表购买。届时若恢复失败，请核对与购买时相同的 Apple账户并使用 App 内恢复入口；取消、等待批准或未验证交易不会授予权益。不要发送 Apple账户密码。参见Apple 恢复购买说明。

Support

Contact QYM

Email levinghost@outlook.com for help or privacy requests. Include the app version, system version, device model, steps and approximate time. Redact private names and values in screenshots. Do not send passwords, complete device identifiers, IDFA, advertising keys or sensitive payment details. The email link opens your email app; you decide whether to send.

Support retention

Support email and attachments are deleted 90 days after the case closes, except where retention is legally required. This rule covers only copies managed by the developer; email providers and backups follow their own arrangements. It does not apply to data held by Unity, Apple or other third parties.

Add, edit and reset

Adding creates a counter immediately. You can then change its name, colour, operation, initial value, target and other options in that counter’s settings. Count using its card or detail controls. A single reset tap asks for confirmation; a double tap resets directly to that counter’s initial value. Read the prompts for reset-all, deletion and history clearing. Resetting does not clear history or delete a counter.

Local data and sharing

Counts and settings stay on this device. There is no separate user account or app-operated cloud sync. Preserve content you need before changing devices, uninstalling or reinstalling; do not assume automatic recovery. You choose whether to share. A counter image is not a complete backup of history and all settings. System backups and recipients’ copies are managed by their respective services.

Reminders, schedules and Widgets

Reminders need system notification permission; counting remains available after refusal. Check system settings for lock-screen display. Scheduled features run under the conditions described in your version; continuous execution while the app is closed is not guaranteed. Where available, Widgets read local shared snapshots and system refresh may be delayed. No advertising or tracking permission is needed. Confirm the value and successful save in the app before reporting an update issue.

Advertising privacy

Advertising is currently disabled; future tests require authorization. Refuse or withdraw through advertising privacy in Settings and continue basic counting without payment. Withdrawal stops subsequent app-owned requests and refresh and destroys the current banner. Request deletion of vendor-held data separately. ATT and advertising consent are independent; system tracking settings do not replace advertising consent. See the privacy policy, Unity’s privacy policy and Apple’s tracking guidance.

Premium purchases and restore

Real purchasing is not yet open, and there is no Premium-account login. When available, non-consumable Premium access will use Apple’s native purchase and restore flow. Test displays do not grant purchase rights. For restore, use the same Apple Account as the purchase and the app’s restore control. Cancellation, pending approval and unverified transactions do not grant access. Never send your Apple Account password. See Apple’s restore guidance.
