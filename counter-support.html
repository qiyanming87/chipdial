帮助与支持

联系 QYM

需要帮助或提出隐私请求，请发送邮件至 levinghost@outlook.com。提供 App 版本、系统版本、设备型号、问题步骤和大致发生时间即可。截图请遮盖私人名称与数值；不要发送账号密码或支付资料。邮箱链接只打开邮件应用，由你决定是否发送，不自动附加计数器数据。

新增、修改与重置

新增会直接创建计数器，之后可在设置中修改名称、运算、整数步长、起始值、目标和显示。除法结果须为整数。卡片或详情按钮用于计数；单击重置先确认，双击可直接回到起始值。重置不清除历史或删除计数器。

免费功能与一次性高级版

免费版可创建 5 个计数器，使用默认午夜蓝及按主页顺序显示的小组件。高级版为一次性 Lifetime 购买，不会自动续费；可解锁更多计数器、森林绿/落日橙/曜石黑/泡泡蓝/樱花粉、自动化与提醒，以及单独选择计数器的可选小组件。具体价格以 Apple 在应用中显示为准。高级权益变化不会自动删除已有计数器。

购买与恢复

在“设置 → 查看高级版权益”查看当前 Apple 价格和购买入口；“恢复购买”也在设置中。使用原购买 Apple 账号发起恢复，不需要注册应用账号。购买需 Apple 系统确认，取消、等待批准或未验证交易不会解锁。若暂时不能读取商品，可使用“重新加载商品”查看结果；不会因为打开页面或重试读取而自动购买。可另参阅 Apple 恢复购买说明。

如何启用 iCloud 合并备份？

在“设置 → iCloud 数据同步”启用。默认关闭，先查看合并预览并确认处理方式；可合并本机与云端数据，或采用当前同步空间的云端数据。使用同一 iCloud 账号的 iPhone/iPad 可合并数值、名称、顺序、配置与保留历史。账号或空间变化需重新选择，旧空间数据不会自动上传到新空间。

数据进入你的私人 CloudKit 数据库，占用 Apple 管理的 iCloud 空间，不上传到开发者服务器。此功能本身不另收费，也不会授予或恢复高级权益。主页布局、当前选择、全局数字动画、权限和自动化规则保持设备设置；旧计划在空间变化后暂停。更新受系统调度和网络影响，不保证即时完成。

如何删除数据和管理备份？

重置、删除计数器和清空历史是不同操作。删除本机计数器不自动删除其他设备保留的数据或云端备份；清空历史不等于彻底清除所有恢复副本和云端备份。可在 iCloud 数据同步页面分别查看本机恢复记录和云端备份。删除本机恢复记录只影响这台设备的恢复列表。

云端备份清理只处理确认时选择的云端内容，不删除本机计数器或其他设备数据；这台设备会暂停自动上传。之后手动合并或其他设备备份时，仍保留的数据可再次上传。关闭同步或删除 App 不等于清除所有云端、系统备份和已分享副本。

Apple Watch 如何保存与同步？

手表可独立新建和操作本机计数器，免费可创建 5 个。与配对 iPhone 的镜像计数通过配对连接传输，离线操作先保存在手表。手表本机计数器只在你明确选择迁移且 iPhone 确认保存后转为镜像，并保留迁移前副本。iPad 不直接连接 Apple Watch；可先通过同一 iCloud 账号与配对 iPhone 合并，再由 iPhone 连接手表。高级版使用同一 Lifetime 购买权益。

盘点与 CSV 导出

从主页进入盘点与批次，选择计数器建立独立批次。未清点和确认零分开记录；修正数量需填写原因，有差异时需填写封存说明。封存后只读，重盘会另建批次。盘点不会改写原计数器名称、数值或历史，也不通过 Watch 或小组件同步。

封存后可生成核对表和操作记录两个 CSV。它们包含批次/物品名称、数量、差异、时间、更正原因、封存说明及本地标识。系统面板由你选择保存或分享，打开面板不会自动发送。成功生成的 CSV 临时副本当前没有固定自动清理周期。

分享图片与历史归档

分享应用只包含介绍和 App Store 链接。单卡 PNG 包含名称、数值、运算、起始值及已开启目标，不含完整历史。生成新图片时会尝试清理本应用超过 48 小时的旧临时 PNG，接收方副本不受影响。

iCloud 数据同步页面可生成并主动保存或分享历史归档，包含升级时保留的历史、后续记录及这台设备保留的首次同步和原空间副本；旧版本已经裁剪的历史无法补回。分享前请确认接收方。

提醒、自动化与小组件

通知需要系统许可，拒绝后仍可基本计数；提醒内容可能显示计数器名称。高级版可配置周期操作和提醒；计数操作在应用再次运行时可补执行，不保证关闭应用后持续执行。计划规则不会随计数器复制给其他设备。

排序小组件按主页顺序显示前 1/2/3 个计数器。iOS 17 及以上可直接使用加减按钮；iOS 16 点击打开相应计数器。高级版可选小组件支持选择其他计数器。系统控制小组件刷新；遇到未更新，请先在 App 确认数值和保存结果。应用无广告，不需要广告跟踪许可。

支持资料保存

支持问题结案后 90 天删除相关邮件及附件，法律要求保留的资料除外。此规则仅适用于开发者管理的副本；邮件服务商、系统备份和 Apple 等服务按各自范围处理。完整数据处理说明请阅读隐私政策。

Support

Contact QYM

Email levinghost@outlook.com for help or privacy requests. Include the app version, system version, device model, steps and approximate time. Redact private names and values in screenshots; do not send account passwords or payment information. The link opens your email app, and you decide whether to send. Counter data is not automatically attached.

Add, edit and reset

Adding creates a counter immediately. Change its name, operation, integer step, initial value, target and display in its settings. Division must produce a whole number. Count using its card or detail controls. A single reset tap asks for confirmation; a double tap returns directly to the initial value. Resetting does not clear history or delete a counter.

Free features and one-time Premium

The free app allows creation of 5 counters, the default Midnight Blue appearance and widgets ordered like Home. Premium is a one-time Lifetime purchase without automatic renewal. It adds more counters, Forest Green/Sunset Orange/Obsidian Black/Bubble Blue/Cherry Blossom Pink, automation/reminders and optional widgets that let you select another counter. Apple's displayed in-app price is authoritative. Changes in Premium access do not automatically delete existing counters.

Purchase and restore

Open Settings > View Premium benefits for Apple's current price and purchase controls. Restore Purchases is also in Settings. Use the original purchasing Apple Account; no app-specific account is needed. Apple asks for purchase confirmation. Cancelled, pending or unverified transactions do not unlock Premium. If product information cannot be read, use Reload product to see the result. Opening the page or retrying a product lookup does not automatically make a purchase. See Apple's restore-purchase guidance.

How do I enable iCloud merged backups?

Open Settings > iCloud Data Sync. It is off by default. Review the preview and confirm whether to merge local and cloud data or use the current sync space's cloud data. iPhone/iPad devices using the same iCloud account can merge values, names, order, settings and retained history. A changed account or space requires another choice; old-space data is not automatically uploaded to the new space.

Data uses your private CloudKit database and Apple-managed iCloud storage, with no developer-operated server. This feature has no additional app charge and does not grant or restore Premium. Home layout, selection, the global number-animation setting, permissions and automation rules remain device-local; earlier schedules pause after a space change. Updates depend on system scheduling and network conditions, with no guarantee of immediate delivery.

How do I delete data and manage backups?

Resetting, deleting counters and clearing history are different operations. Removing a counter locally does not automatically remove data retained on other devices or cloud backups. Clearing history does not erase every recovery copy or cloud backup. Manage local recovery entries and cloud backups separately on the iCloud Data Sync page. Deleting a local recovery entry only affects this device's recovery list.

Cloud-backup cleanup applies only to the cloud content selected at confirmation. It does not delete local counters or other devices' data and pauses automatic uploads on this device. A later manual merge or another device's backup can upload retained data again. Turning sync off or deleting the app does not clear every cloud copy, system backup or shared copy.

How does Apple Watch save and sync?

The Watch app can create and operate independent local counters, with 5 available free. Mirrors of paired-iPhone counters transfer through the pairing connection; offline actions are saved on the Watch first. A local Watch counter becomes a mirror only after explicit migration and confirmation that the iPhone saved it, with a pre-migration copy retained. iPad does not connect directly to Apple Watch: data can first merge with the paired iPhone through the same iCloud account, then transfer through that iPhone's Watch connection. Premium uses the same Lifetime purchase.

Inventory batches and CSV export

Open inventory/batches from Home and select counters to create a separate batch. Uncounted items and confirmed zeroes are distinct. Corrections require a reason, and discrepancies require a completion note. Closed batches are read-only; recounting creates another batch. Inventory does not change original counter names, values or history and does not sync through Watch or widgets.

After closing a batch, export a check sheet and an operation history as two CSV files. They include batch/item names, quantities, differences, times, correction reasons, completion notes and local identifiers. Choose whether to save or share in the system sheet; opening it does not send files automatically. Successfully generated temporary CSVs currently have no fixed automatic cleanup schedule.

Share images and history archives

Sharing the app includes its introduction and App Store link. A counter PNG contains its name, value, operation, initial value and enabled target, not full history. Generating a new image attempts to clean this app's old temporary PNGs after 48 hours; recipients' copies are unaffected.

The iCloud Data Sync page lets you generate and choose to save/share a history archive. It includes history retained at the upgrade, later records and first-sync/previous-space copies kept on this device. Older-version history already pruned cannot be recovered. Check the recipient before sharing.

Reminders, automation and widgets

Notifications require system permission, while basic counting remains available after refusal. A reminder can display the counter name. Premium can configure recurring operations and reminders. Counter operations can catch up when the app runs again; continuous operation while the app is closed is not guaranteed. Schedule rules are not copied to other devices with counters.

Ordered widgets show the first 1, 2 or 3 Home counters. On iOS 17 or later, use their plus/minus controls directly; on iOS 16, tap to open the counter. Premium optional widgets can select another counter. The system controls widget refresh timing. Confirm the value and successful save in the app before reporting an update issue. The app has no ads and needs no advertising-tracking permission.

Support retention

Support email and attachments are deleted 90 days after the case closes, except where retention is legally required. This rule covers developer-managed copies; email providers, system backups, Apple and other services manage their information separately.
