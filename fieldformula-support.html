FieldFormula Support

FieldFormula is an offline reference calculator for building, renovation, woodworking, electrical, HVAC, and plumbing estimates.

How to use

Select a trade category, enter project values, and review the reference estimate. Projects and history are stored locally on your device.

Important note

Results are starting points for professional review. Check manufacturer data, project specifications, site conditions, and applicable requirements before making decisions.
