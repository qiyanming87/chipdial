隐私政策

本地计数与设置

QYM 提供原生 iPhone / iPad「计数器」。名称、数值、颜色、运算、目标、排序、历史和设置保存在本机，用于计数与恢复状态；每个计数器保留最近1000条历史。本应用没有自建账号或云同步服务。归零不清除历史；删除计数器和清除历史需分别操作。

小组件与提醒

提供小组件时，应用通过系统保护的 App Group 共享本地计数快照和高级权益状态。提醒在你允许通知后使用系统本地通知；名称与任务内容可能出现在通知中。拒绝通知不影响基本计数。计划功能按该版本提供的条件执行，不保证应用关闭期间持续运行。

主动分享

你决定是否通过系统分享面板发送内容。单卡图片可含名称、数值、运算、起始值和目标，不含完整历史。生成新图片时，应用尝试清理本机临时目录中超过48小时的旧分享图片；接收方的副本不受此清理控制。

广告及相关数据

当前广告未启用，后续仅经授权测试。广告组件包括 Unity LevelPlay/ironSource、Unity Ads 及随附 Ad Quality。启用后可能处理设备与应用信息、设备标识、IP及大致地区、广告互动、性能和诊断数据，用于投放、个性化、衡量、防欺诈及质量分析，并读取或写入广告相关设备信息。参见Unity 隐私政策及Ad Quality 说明。

Unity 接收方与跨境处理

Unity 的公开政策由 Unity Technologies S.F. 负责，欧洲联系主体为 Unity Technologies ApS。相关广告服务可自行决定数据用途和处理方式，作为独立数据控制者处理资料。数据可能提供给关联方（含 ironSource）和服务商，在美国、以色列或其它设施及服务商所在国家处理。Unity 对相关欧洲跨境传输说明采用适当性决定或标准合同条款等保障。这是厂商可能的安排，不表示本应用已发生这些传输。详见Unity 隐私政策、数据处理附录和广告条款。

Unity 保存与数据请求

Unity 按用途、服务关系、法律及防欺诈需要保存资料，不适用支持邮件90天规则。可向 DPO@unity3d.com 提出访问、更正、删除或退出等请求；可能需要身份核验，部分资料可能依法保留。如 Unity Ads 广告提供「Data Privacy」图标，也可从该入口办理，具体方式见其政策。

同意、拒绝与撤回

App 获得有效的广告处理及设备信息访问同意后才启动广告。你通过应用提供的广告隐私选择明确接受、拒绝或撤回。选择、对应政策版本和选择时间保存在本机，用于记住选择与核对授权；这份记录不含 IDFA。接受包含上述用途；在适用地区，我们据此记录 GDPR 广告处理同意，并设置 CCPA 出售／共享退出选项。拒绝或撤回同时退出相关出售／共享和跨情境广告用途，仍可免费基本计数。在设置的广告隐私入口可修改选择或撤回，App 会停止后续请求与刷新、销毁当前横幅并丢弃过期回调。撤回不自动删除厂商已收到的数据，也不保证已启动的 SDK 反初始化。

系统跟踪选择

Apple ATT 跟踪许可与广告同意分别管理，彼此不能替代。基本计数无需提供广告标识。可在系统「隐私与安全性 → 跟踪」修改许可，参见Apple 跟踪权限说明。

正式购买尚未开放。将来开放非消耗型高级版购买与恢复时，由 Apple 处理支付，App 以已验证交易确认权益，不读取银行卡资料，也不创建高级版登录账号。测试展示不代表真实付费权益。

支持问题结案后90天删除相关邮件及附件，法律要求保留的资料除外。此规则仅适用于开发者管理的副本；邮件服务商和备份按各自安排处理，不适用于 Unity、Apple 或其他第三方持有的资料。

Privacy policy

Local counters and settings

QYM provides the native iPhone / iPad Counter app. Names, values, colours, operations, targets, ordering, history and settings are stored on your device to support counting and restore state. Each counter keeps its latest 1000 history events. There is no app-operated account or cloud sync. Resetting a value does not clear history; counter deletion and history clearing are separate actions.

Widgets and reminders

Where available, Widgets receive local counter snapshots and Premium status through a system-protected App Group. Reminders use local system notifications with your permission; names and task information may appear in them. Refusal leaves basic counting available. Scheduled features run under the conditions described in your version; they are not a promise of continuous execution while the app is closed.

Sharing

You choose whether to send content through the system share sheet. A counter image may show its name, value, operation, initial value and target, but not complete history. When generating a new image, the app attempts to remove its temporary share images older than 48 hours. This does not remove recipients’ copies.

Advertising data

Advertising is currently disabled; future tests require authorization. Components include Unity LevelPlay/ironSource, Unity Ads and bundled Ad Quality. When enabled, they may process device/app information, identifiers, IP-derived broad location, ad interactions, performance and diagnostics, and access advertising-related device storage for delivery, personalisation, measurement, fraud prevention and quality analysis. See Unity’s privacy policy and the Ad Quality documentation.

Unity recipients and international processing

Unity’s policy identifies Unity Technologies S.F. as responsible, with Unity Technologies ApS as its European contact. Relevant advertising services can act as independent data controllers. Data may reach affiliates, including ironSource, and service providers in the US, Israel or other facility/provider countries. Unity describes adequacy decisions or standard contractual clauses for relevant European transfers. These possible vendor arrangements do not establish transfers by this app. See Unity’s policy, DPA and advertising terms.

Unity retention and requests

Unity retains data according to purposes, service relationships, legal and fraud-prevention needs, rather than our 90-day support rule. Send access, correction, deletion or opt-out requests to DPO@unity3d.com. Identity checks and legal retention exceptions may apply. Where a Unity Ads ad offers a Data Privacy icon, that entry is another option; consult its policy for details.

Consent and withdrawal

The app waits for valid advertising-processing and device-access consent before starting ads. You explicitly accept, refuse or withdraw through the app’s advertising privacy choice. The choice, matching policy version and decision time are stored locally to remember your choice and check authorization; this record contains no IDFA. Acceptance covers the purposes above. Where applicable, this choice records GDPR advertising consent and sets CCPA sale/sharing opt-out choices accordingly. Refusal or withdrawal opts out of related sale/sharing and cross-context advertising, without payment, and leaves basic counting available. Change or withdraw the choice through advertising privacy in Settings. The app stops subsequent requests and refresh, destroys its current banner and discards stale callbacks. This does not automatically delete vendor-held data or guarantee that an already started SDK is uninitialized.

Apple tracking permission

Apple ATT and advertising consent are separate choices; neither replaces the other. Basic counting does not require an advertising identifier. Change ATT in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. See Apple’s tracking guidance.

Apple purchases

Real purchasing is not yet open. When non-consumable Premium purchases and restore become available, Apple handles payment and the app uses verified transactions to confirm access. It does not read payment-card information or create a Premium login. Test displays do not grant real purchase rights.

Support retention

Support email and attachments are deleted 90 days after the case closes, except where retention is legally required. This rule covers only copies managed by the developer; email providers and backups follow their own arrangements. It does not apply to data held by Unity, Apple or other third parties.

Contact and requests

Contact QYM at levinghost@outlook.com. Your email address, message and attachments are handled by QYM and the email services involved to respond to support or privacy requests. Opening email does not send anything. Request access, correction or deletion of data the developer actually holds; redact sensitive content. Backups, shared copies and Apple/Unity records have separate scopes. Uninstalling cannot guarantee their deletion.
