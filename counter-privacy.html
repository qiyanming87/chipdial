隐私政策

计数与设备设置

数据默认保存在当前设备。开启 iCloud 数据同步后，计数器配置、数值、保留的历史及后续操作会存入你当前 Apple 账号的 CloudKit 私有数据库，占用你的 iCloud 空间。没有应用自建账号。

主页布局、当前选择、全局数字动画和系统权限保留为设备设置。自动化计划绑定本机计数数据空间；切换空间后旧计划暂停，需主动保存未来计划才重新启用。计划规则不会随计数器复制到其他设备。高级版权益由 StoreKit 验证，不作为同步凭证上传。

可选的 iCloud 合并备份

iCloud 数据同步默认关闭。启用后，先查看合并预览，并确认合并本机与云端数据，或采用当前 iCloud 空间的数据。在使用同一 iCloud 账号的 iPhone 和 iPad 上，可合并计数器的数值、名称、顺序、配置及保留的历史。同步空间变化时需重新选择数据处理方式，原空间的数据不会自动混入新空间。

iCloud 合并备份功能本身不另收费，所需 iCloud 账号和可用存储空间由 Apple 管理。后台更新受网络、电量与系统调度影响，不保证即时到达。关闭同步只暂停后续传输，不等于清除云端备份。

恢复、删除与保存范围

首次同步与同步空间切换前会保留本机恢复副本。本机删除计数器或清空历史不会自动删除其他设备保留的数据或云端备份，相关记录仍可能保留在本机恢复入口和云端备份中。可在 iCloud 数据同步页面分别管理本机恢复记录和云端备份；清理云端备份不会删除本机计数器，仍由各设备保留的数据可再次合并备份。归档包含本次升级时仍保留的历史及后续记录，无法找回旧版本已裁剪的历史。手动导出后由你在系统面板中选择分享对象。

删除本机恢复记录只清理这台设备的恢复列表，不影响现有计数器、同步历史或云端数据。清理云端备份前会显示确认范围；该操作不删除本机计数器或其他设备数据，并暂停这台设备的自动上传。之后手动合并或其他设备备份时，仍保留的数据可以再次上传；较新云端记录仍需要的关联历史可能继续保留。

重置数值不等于清除历史，删除计数器和清空历史也不是删除所有恢复副本或云端备份。系统“删除 App”与“卸载 App”对本地数据的处理不同；删除 App 不保证删除 iCloud、系统备份、你已导出的文件或接收方的副本。

Apple Watch

Apple Watch 通过配对的 iPhone 传输计数器及操作。手表本机计数器默认独立保存；只有明确选择迁移并收到 iPhone 保存确认后才转为镜像，并保留迁移前副本。iPhone 启用 iCloud 同步后，迁移后的数值、配置和当前保留的历史可继续同步到同一 iCloud 账号的 iPhone 和 iPad。开发者不设置自己的数据服务器。

小组件、通知与计划

小组件在配置共享权限后读取本应用的计数器快照。系统提醒需要你主动允许通知；自定义触感不请求权限。

小组件通过本应用的共享数据读取计数快照和所需的有效高级版状态。通知中可能出现你设置的计数器名称和提醒内容；拒绝通知不影响基本计数。周期计数操作在应用可运行时执行或补执行，不保证应用关闭期间持续运行。

盘点与批次

盘点批次在当前设备独立保存，包括批次和物品名称、预期与实盘数量、清点状态、上轮数量、操作时间与前后数量、更正原因和封存说明。批次还保存用于关联项目与重盘的本地标识。

批次物品名称仅用于本批次，不修改原计数器名称、数值或历史。盘点批次当前不通过 Apple Watch 或小组件同步，也不主动上传到开发者服务器。

封存后，原批次数量与说明保持只读。重盘会另建批次并保留上轮记录。系统备份、恢复及你主动保存或分享的副本由相应系统、服务或接收方管理。

Apple 购买与高级版

内购由 Apple StoreKit 处理，本应用不读取银行卡资料。高级版依据已验证的购买权益启用；购买和恢复不会为你建立应用账号。

基本计数免费，免费版可创建 5 个计数器。可选高级版为一次性 Lifetime 非消耗型购买，不是自动续费订阅；价格以应用内 Apple 显示为准。高级版提供更多计数器、额外皮肤、自动化与提醒及可单独选择计数器的小组件。已购用户可使用同一购买 Apple 账号发起恢复。

购买需你在 Apple 系统界面确认；取消、等待批准或未验证的交易不会授予高级权益。购买被退款或撤销时，依据 Apple 的有效验证结果更新高级权限，不因此自动删除已有计数器。同步计数数据不会购买、转移或恢复高级权益。Apple 的账户、付款和交易信息按其服务政策处理，参见 Apple 隐私政策。

主动分享与导出

应用分享包含应用介绍和 App Store 链接，不包含计数器数据。单卡分享生成包含名称、数值、运算、起始值及已启用目标的 PNG，打开系统面板后由你选择是否分享。计数图片临时文件会按保留期限清理。

盘点导出会在本机生成核对表和操作记录两个 CSV，包含物品与批次名称、数量、差异、时间、更正原因、封存说明及本地标识。你在系统面板中选择是否保存或分享；打开面板不会自动发送。成功生成的 CSV 临时副本目前没有固定自动清理周期。

生成新的单卡图片时，应用尝试清理自身临时分享目录中超过 48 小时的旧 PNG；接收方保存的副本不受此清理控制。历史归档也可能包含这台设备保留的首次同步及原同步空间副本，请在系统分享面板中自行选择接收方。

广告与外部服务

本版本没有广告，不运行第三方广告或广告跟踪 SDK。基本计数、小组件和 iCloud 功能不需要广告跟踪许可。你主动使用的 Apple 系统服务、设备备份、邮件和分享接收服务分别按其自身政策处理资料。

Privacy policy

Counters and device settings

Data is saved on this device by default. Enabling iCloud data sync stores counter settings, values, retained history and later operations in your current Apple Account's private CloudKit database, using your iCloud storage. No app account is created.

The Home layout, selected counter, global number-animation switch and system permissions stay on each device. Automation rules bind to the local counter data space. After switching spaces, earlier rules pause until explicitly saved with a future schedule. Rules are not copied to other devices with counters. Premium access is verified through StoreKit and is not uploaded as a sync credential.

Optional iCloud merged backups

iCloud data sync is off by default. After enabling it, review the preview and confirm whether to merge local and cloud data or use the current iCloud space's data. Values, names, ordering, settings and retained history can be merged between iPhone and iPad devices using the same iCloud account. A changed sync space requires a new data-handling choice; previous-space data is not automatically mixed into it.

The iCloud merge-backup feature has no additional app charge. Apple manages the iCloud account and available storage. Background updates depend on network, power and system scheduling and are not guaranteed to arrive immediately. Turning sync off pauses transfers rather than clearing cloud backups.

Recovery, deletion and retained copies

A local recovery copy is kept before first sync and before switching sync spaces. Deleting a counter or clearing history on this device does not automatically delete data retained on other devices or cloud backups; related records may remain in local recovery and cloud backups. Manage local recovery records and cloud backups separately on the iCloud data sync page. Cleaning cloud backups does not delete local counters, and data retained on each device can be backed up again. Archives include history retained at this upgrade and later records; history pruned by older versions cannot be recovered. After exporting, you choose a recipient in the system share sheet.

Deleting local recovery entries only clears this device's recovery list; it does not change current counters, synchronized history or cloud data. Cloud-backup cleanup asks you to confirm its scope. It does not delete local counters or other devices' data and pauses automatic uploads on this device. A later manual merge or another device's backup can upload retained data again; related history needed by newer cloud records may remain.

Resetting a value is not clearing history. Deleting a counter or clearing history does not erase all recovery copies or cloud backups. Delete App and Offload App handle local data differently. Deleting the app does not guarantee removal of iCloud data, system backups, exported files or recipients' copies.

Apple Watch

Apple Watch transfers counters and actions through its paired iPhone. Local watch counters are kept separately by default. They become mirrors only after you explicitly choose to migrate and the iPhone confirms saving them; a pre-migration copy is kept. If iCloud sync is enabled on iPhone, migrated values, settings and currently retained history can sync to iPhone and iPad devices using the same iCloud account. The developer does not operate a separate data server.

Widgets, notifications and schedules

Widgets read counter snapshots after shared access is configured. System reminders require your notification permission; custom haptics do not request permission.

Widgets read counter snapshots and necessary verified Premium state through this app's shared data. Notifications may display the counter name and reminder text you configured. Refusing notifications leaves basic counting available. Scheduled counter operations execute or catch up when the app can run; continuous execution while the app is closed is not guaranteed.

Inventory batches

Inventory batches are saved separately on this device, including batch and item names, expected and actual quantities, counted status, previous-round quantities, operation times and quantity changes, correction reasons and completion notes. Local identifiers link items and recounts.

Batch item names apply only to that batch and do not change the original counter names, values or history. Inventory batches currently do not sync through Apple Watch or widgets and are not automatically uploaded to developer servers.

After a batch is closed, its quantities and notes are read-only. A recount creates a separate batch and retains the previous round. System backups, restoration and copies you choose to save or share are managed by the relevant system, service or recipient.

Apple purchases and Premium

Apple StoreKit handles purchases. This app does not read payment card details. Premium requires verified purchase access; buying or restoring does not create an app account.

Basic counting is free, with creation of up to 5 counters. Optional Premium is a one-time Lifetime non-consumable purchase, not an auto-renewing subscription. Apple's price shown in the app is authoritative. Premium includes more counters, extra appearances, automation/reminders and widgets that let you select another counter individually. Existing purchasers can request restoration using the same purchasing Apple Account.

You confirm purchases in Apple's system interface. Cancelled, pending or unverified transactions do not grant Premium. Refunds or revocation update access according to Apple's verified state without automatically deleting existing counters. Syncing counter data does not buy, transfer or restore Premium. Apple's account, payment and transaction information is governed by its service policies; see Apple's privacy policy.

Sharing and exports you choose

App sharing includes an introduction and an App Store link, without counter data. Sharing a counter creates a PNG with its name, value, operation, starting value and enabled target. You choose whether to share in the system panel. Temporary counter images are cleaned up after their retention period.

Inventory export creates two CSV files on this device: a check sheet and an operation history. Together they include item and batch names, quantities, differences, times, correction reasons, completion notes and local identifiers. You choose whether to save or share them in the system sheet; opening the sheet does not send them automatically. Successfully generated temporary CSV copies currently have no fixed automatic cleanup schedule.

When generating a new counter image, the app attempts to clean its own temporary share PNGs older than 48 hours. This does not remove recipients' copies. A history archive may also contain first-sync and previous-space copies retained on this device. Choose recipients yourself in the system share sheet.

Advertising and external services

This version has no ads and does not run third-party advertising or advertising-tracking SDKs. Basic counting, widgets and iCloud features do not require advertising-tracking permission. Apple services, device backups, email services and share destinations you choose handle information under their respective policies.

Contact and support data

Contact QYM at levinghost@outlook.com. The email address, message and attachments you choose to send are used to answer support or privacy requests. Opening email does not send it or automatically attach counter data. Request handling or deletion of support information the developer actually holds; do not send Apple Account passwords or payment information.

Support email and attachments are deleted 90 days after the case closes, except where retention is legally required. This rule covers copies managed by the developer; email providers, system backups, Apple and other services handle their information separately.
