中文

就寝提醒是一款个人就寝习惯工具。App 无需注册账户，不接入广告或分析 SDK；不把下列本机运行资料上传给开发者，也不由开发者建立用户数据服务器。

保存在设备上的资料

  • 提醒时间、提醒范围、开关、延后排程、通知标识，以及排程、解除遮罩和到期处理所需的运行记录。
  • 你确认或手动补记的就寝时间、当晚目标、日期、记录来源和时区。日历和小组件使用这些本地记录及其汇总结果。
  • 语言偏好及其他必要的本地设置。
  • 免费试用起始、截止、到期状态及必要的时间核验信息。主 App 使用这台设备的系统钥匙串保存试用资格，并提供本地共享副本供提醒扩展判断可用性。
  • Apple 已验证的订阅商品、交易标识、权益状态与截止时间等必要信息，供 App 和提醒扩展在本机判断订阅是否有效。

屏幕使用时间与就寝记录

授权和应用选择通过 Apple 系统界面完成。App 保存所需的不透明系统标识来控制支持范围内的遮罩，不读取所选应用的内容、浏览内容或全手机最后操作时间。提醒和遮罩可关闭或解除。

就寝记录来自你确认准备就寝或手动录入的时间，不代表系统测量的实际睡眠。未回应提醒不会自动生成记录。你可以在日历中修改或删除记录；修改提醒时间会清除受影响的当前晚记录，已结束的历史夜晚保留。

免费试用与 Apple 订阅

新用户可手动开始15天本机免费试用，无需确认订阅，不会自动扣费。资格按设备记录，不绑定开发者账户或 Apple 账户；卸载重装不重新开始。已开始的试用保留原截止时间。

你另行确认的月订阅由 Apple 的 StoreKit 和 App Store 处理，可通过相同购买 Apple 账户恢复。支付资料和 Apple 账户认证由 Apple 处理；App 不收集银行卡资料，也不会向开发者上传本地交易记录。App 只保存判断权益所需的信息。

通知、存储与删除

提醒使用设备上的本地通知，不向开发者发送推送请求。你可以在系统设置撤销通知或屏幕使用时间权限。设置、日历、扩展和小组件共享资料存放于 App 的本地容器；没有开发者提供的跨设备日历同步。

删除日历记录会从 App 的日历存储中移除该记录。卸载 App 会移除其普通本地资料，但试用资格保留在系统钥匙串，因此重装不会领取第二次试用。取消 Apple 订阅不会删除日历；订阅的管理与交易保留由 Apple 处理。

若你主动发送邮件，邮件内容及发件地址会由你使用的邮件服务发送给开发者，仅用于答复你的咨询或处理反馈。App 只帮助打开邮件应用或复制邮箱，不会自动发送邮件。联系：levinghost@outlook.com。

English

Bedtime Reminder is a personal bedtime-routine app. It needs no app account and contains no advertising or analytics SDKs. The following on-device operational data is not uploaded to the developer; the developer does not run a user-data server for this app.

Data stored on your device

  • Reminder time and scope, switches, snooze schedules, notification identifiers and operational records needed to schedule reminders, dismiss shields and handle expiration.
  • Bedtimes you confirm or enter, nightly goals, dates, entry sources and time zones. The calendar and widgets use these local entries and their summaries.
  • Your language preference and other necessary local settings.
  • Trial start, deadline, expiration state and necessary time-validation information. The main app stores trial eligibility in this device's system Keychain and shares a local access copy with reminder extensions.
  • Necessary Apple-verified product and transaction identifiers, subscription-access state and expiration information, used by the app and reminder extensions to check access locally.

Screen Time and bedtime entries

Apple's system interfaces handle authorization and app selection. The app stores necessary opaque system identifiers to apply shields within the supported scope. It does not read selected-app content, browsing content or the last interaction time across your phone. Reminders can be turned off and shields dismissed.

Entries come from a time you confirm as ready for bed or enter manually. They are not measured sleep. An unanswered reminder creates no entry. You can edit or delete entries in the calendar. Changing the reminder time clears affected current-night entries; completed past nights are kept.

Free trial and Apple subscriptions

New users can choose to start a 15-day local trial without confirming a subscription. It never charges automatically. Eligibility is stored per device, without a developer account or Apple Account link. Reinstalling does not restart it; an existing trial keeps its original deadline.

A monthly subscription you separately confirm is handled by Apple's StoreKit and App Store, and can be restored using the purchasing Apple Account. Apple handles payment details and Apple Account authentication. The app does not collect card information or upload local transaction records to the developer. It stores only information needed to check access.

Notifications, storage and deletion

Reminders use local notifications on your device and do not request push delivery from the developer. Revoke notification or Screen Time permissions in system settings. Settings, calendar entries and data shared with extensions and widgets remain in the app's local containers. The developer provides no cross-device calendar sync.

Deleting a calendar entry removes it from the app's calendar storage. Uninstalling removes ordinary app data, while trial eligibility remains in the system Keychain; reinstalling does not grant a second trial. Cancelling an Apple subscription does not delete the calendar. Apple manages subscriptions and retains its transaction records.

Contacting the developer

If you choose to email the developer, your email service sends your message and sender address. They are used to answer your request or address your feedback. The app only opens a mail app or copies the address; it never sends mail automatically. Contact: levinghost@outlook.com.
