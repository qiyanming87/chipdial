中文隐私政策

适用范围与开发者

本政策适用于 iPhone 和 iPad 上的“就寝提醒”及其提醒扩展和小组件。开发者联系方式：levinghost@outlook.com。App 无需注册开发者账户，没有开发者提供的用户数据服务器或跨设备日历同步。

每日提醒、屏幕使用时间遮罩、就寝日历与小组件免费使用；可选的 Apple 月订阅仅解锁额外皮肤。使用皮肤不要求授予屏幕使用时间或通知权限。

提醒设置与本地通知

你设置的提醒时间、开关、范围模式、延后与解除状态、排程标识和必要时间信息保存在设备上，用于安排或取消提醒、处理“10 分钟后再提醒”与避免重复操作。允许通知后，App 通过 Apple 系统在本机安排通知；没有开发者远程推送服务。

App 读取系统返回的通知授权和显示选项，用于显示权限状态、恢复排程和排查问题。通知是否在锁屏、横幅或通知中心出现，取决于你的系统设置、专注模式与系统调度。

屏幕使用时间与应用范围

屏幕使用时间访问由你在 Apple 的系统授权界面决定。App 保存“所有类别”范围模式，或你通过系统选择器选定的应用、类别和网站的不透明系统标识，用于在受支持范围显示和解除就寝遮罩。主 App 与提醒扩展通过设备上的 App Group 共享这些必要设置。

App 不读取所选应用里的内容、网页浏览内容、完整使用历史或全设备最后操作时间。它不以屏幕使用时间推断你是否睡着。现版本开启每日提醒计划需要屏幕使用时间授权；拒绝或撤销此权限会影响每日提醒与遮罩计划。日历、手动记录和皮肤仍可使用，且不要求此权限。

就寝记录与小组件

就寝记录来自你点击“我准备睡觉了”或手动录入，包括准备就寝时间、当晚目标、夜晚日期、时区、记录时间和来源。达标或晚睡状态仅比较记录时间与目标，不是睡眠检测或医学测量。没有回应提醒不会自动生成记录。凌晨 00:00–05:59 的目标归前一晚。

日历和小组件使用这些记录及其本地汇总。主 App 向 App Group 写入供小组件读取的显示快照，不向小组件提供应用选择标识或完整交易记录。你可编辑或删除记录；修改提醒时间会清除受影响的当前晚记录，已结束的历史夜晚保留。

语言与皮肤偏好

语言选择、所选皮肤和额外皮肤权益的已验证起止时间保存在设备的独立偏好文件中，供主 App、提醒扩展和小组件一致显示。小组件与遮罩只读取必要的外观权益投影，不读取 Apple 账户、支付资料或完整交易记录。

经典紫始终免费。其他四款皮肤由有效 Apple 月订阅解锁；订阅失效或退款后恢复经典紫，并可保留你原先选择以便权益恢复后使用。修改语言或皮肤不会修改你的日历记录或提醒时间。

Apple 购买与交易信息

只有你另行确认购买，Apple 才会处理月订阅。Apple 负责 Apple 账户认证、支付、续费、取消和其交易记录的保留。App 不接收 Apple 账户密码、银行卡资料或完整账户资料。经 StoreKit 验证的商品与交易标识、交易环境、有效期、撤销和宽限期状态等必要信息在本机缓存，用于验证、恢复和显示皮肤权益，不决定免费提醒或屏幕使用时间功能是否可用。

商品价格、币种和商店地区等系统返回信息用于显示本地价格和购买状态，也可能包含在本地诊断快照中。取消或管理订阅可从 App 的订阅页面进入 Apple 系统流程；取消不会删除就寝日历。Apple 的系统服务另按 Apple 的隐私政策处理数据：Apple Privacy Policy。

旧版本试用记录

旧版本曾在本机记录试用起始、截止、到期状态及时间核验信息，并在这台设备的系统钥匙串保存试用锚点，在 App Group 保存本地副本。现版本不要求开始试用，也不再用这些旧记录限制免费提醒或遮罩；它们不会解锁付费皮肤。

旧钥匙串记录未同步到开发者服务器，不绑定开发者账户，也不作为跨设备试用资格。该记录可能在删除 App 后继续留在系统钥匙串；App 当前没有单独删除此旧钥匙串记录的入口，不能保证重装会清除它。

本地运行记录与未使用的数据服务

为核对排程、权限变化、延后性能和购买状态，App 与扩展在本机保存运行事件及最近状态快照。内容包括时间、配置状态、权限结果、选择数量、排程标识、耗时和前述必要订阅/旧试用状态；性能日志也使用系统日志。运行事件不记录所选应用名称、应用内容或选择标识本身。

App 没有广告或第三方分析 SDK，不使用广告跟踪标识，不接入开发者的上传接口或云同步，不自动向开发者传送这些资料。App 不请求健康、相机、麦克风、通讯录或定位权限。Apple 的操作系统诊断、App Store、系统备份以及你主动使用的外部服务，分别由相应系统设置和服务政策管理。

保存位置与期限

提醒设置、应用范围、日历、显示快照、偏好及权益缓存保存在 App 的本地容器或仅供本 App 及扩展使用的 App Group；旧试用锚点保存在设备钥匙串。支持保护属性的文件使用系统文件保护。系统备份可能包含部分 App 数据，取决于设备备份设置；App 没有自行提供云端数据副本。

日历和偏好不按固定天数自动删除，保留到你修改、逐条删除记录或删除 App。诊断状态快照随运行覆盖更新；事件日志按大小轮替（约 128 KB 上限，压缩时保留较新的约 96 KB），没有固定的按天保留期限。旧试用与交易权益缓存可为兼容或核验保留，不代表持续有效权益。Apple 保存的交易、系统日志和备份期限由 Apple 及你的设置决定。

修改、删除与请求处理

你可以在就寝日历打开日期详情，编辑或删除该晚的记录；显示快照将在后续刷新时同步更新，小组件的可见刷新由系统安排。在 App 设置中可更改提醒范围、语言和皮肤；关闭提醒会停止本 App 后续提醒排程并解除其遮罩，但不会删除历史日历或全部诊断记录。App 当前没有“一键清除全部本地数据”功能。

如需移除普通 App 数据，可在系统“设置 → 通用 → iPhone/iPad 储存空间 → 就寝提醒”选择“删除 App”；“卸载 App”可能保留文稿和数据。删除 App 不等于取消 Apple 订阅，也不保证删除旧钥匙串记录、系统备份或 Apple 交易记录。开发者无法远程访问或删除你设备上的本地资料。若你曾主动发送邮件，可联系 levinghost@outlook.com 请求处理或删除开发者持有的邮件；请求不需要附带应用使用记录、Apple 密码或支付资料。

撤销权限与停止使用

屏幕使用时间：在系统“设置 → 屏幕使用时间 → 有屏幕使用时间访问权限的 App”关闭“就寝提醒”（具体菜单名称随系统版本变化）。系统将撤销相关访问，遮罩功能停止；App 再次获知撤权时会暂停每日提醒计划、取消其排程和通知，并更新状态。撤权不会自动删除本地设置和日历，日历、手动记录和皮肤仍可使用。

通知：在系统“设置 → 通知 → 就寝提醒”关闭“允许通知”，或从 App 的通知设置入口进入系统页面。此操作停止系统通知显示，不自动撤销已批准的屏幕使用时间遮罩或删除本地记录。你也可在主页关闭每日提醒来停止排程和遮罩。取消皮肤订阅需通过 Apple 的订阅管理，不会自动撤销上述权限。

邮件、分享与外部页面

“联系开发者”只打开你选择的邮件应用或复制邮箱；邮件必须由你主动确认发送，不会自动附加本地诊断或就寝记录。你发送的地址、正文和附件会由相关邮件服务及开发者的 Outlook 邮箱处理，仅用于答复、排查你反馈的问题和处理请求，不用于广告或出售。邮件在处理咨询所需期间保留；你可请求删除，法律必须保留的资料及邮件服务的备份规则另行适用。

分享应用会将商店链接交给你选择的分享目标；评价应用会打开 Apple 页面。公开支持/隐私网页由 GitHub Pages 托管，访问时该服务可能处理 IP 地址等标准网络请求信息。这些由你选择的外部应用或服务依其政策处理数据，App 不把本地日历、范围标识或交易缓存自动发送给它们。联系：levinghost@outlook.com。

Privacy policy in English

Scope and developer

This policy covers Bedtime Reminder on iPhone and iPad, its reminder extensions and widgets. Contact the developer at levinghost@outlook.com. The app requires no developer account. The developer provides no user-data server or cross-device calendar sync.

Daily reminders, Screen Time shields, the bedtime calendar and widgets are free. An optional Apple monthly subscription unlocks additional skins only. Using skins does not require Screen Time or notification permission.

Reminder settings and local notifications

Your reminder time, switches, scope mode, snooze and dismissal state, schedule identifiers and necessary timing information are stored on the device to schedule or cancel reminders, handle a 10-minute snooze and prevent duplicate actions. With notification permission, the app schedules local notifications through Apple's system. There is no developer-operated remote push service.

The app reads notification authorization and presentation settings returned by the system to show permission status, recover schedules and diagnose problems. Lock Screen, banner and Notification Center presentation depends on your settings, Focus and system scheduling.

Screen Time and app scope

You decide whether to grant Screen Time access in Apple's system authorization interface. The app stores the all-categories scope mode or opaque system tokens for apps, categories and websites you select through the system picker, to show and dismiss bedtime shields within the supported scope. The main app and reminder extensions share these necessary settings in an on-device App Group.

The app does not read selected-app content, browsing content, a complete usage history or the device-wide last interaction time. It does not infer sleep from Screen Time. This version requires Screen Time authorization to enable the daily reminder plan. Declining or revoking access affects daily reminder and shield plans. The calendar, manual entries and skins remain available without this permission.

Bedtime entries and widgets

Bedtime entries come from tapping I'm ready for bed or entering a time manually. They include your reported preparation-for-bed time, nightly goal, night date, time zone, recording time and source. On-time or late status only compares the entry with its goal; it is not sleep detection or a medical measurement. An unanswered reminder creates no entry. Goals from 00:00 through 05:59 belong to the previous night.

The calendar and widgets use these entries and local summaries. The main app writes an App Group display snapshot for widgets, without giving widgets app-selection tokens or complete transaction records. You can edit or delete entries. Changing the reminder time clears affected current-night entries; completed past nights are kept.

Language and skin preferences

Your language choice, selected skin and verified start/end dates for additional-skin access are stored in separate on-device preference files for consistent display in the main app, reminder extensions and widgets. Widgets and shields read only the necessary appearance-access projection, not your Apple Account, payment information or complete transaction records.

Classic Purple is always free. The other four skins require an active Apple monthly subscription. When access expires or is refunded, the appearance returns to Classic Purple; your previous choice may be retained for restored access. Changing language or skin does not change calendar entries or your reminder time.

Apple purchases and transaction information

Apple handles a monthly subscription only after you separately confirm a purchase. Apple manages Apple Account authentication, payment, renewal, cancellation and retention of its transaction records. The app does not receive your Apple Account password, card details or full account information. Necessary StoreKit-verified product and transaction identifiers, transaction environment, expiration, revocation and grace-period status are cached locally to verify, restore and display skin access. They do not determine access to free reminders or Screen Time features.

System-provided price, currency and storefront information is used to show local pricing and purchase status and may appear in local diagnostic snapshots. The subscription page opens Apple's system flow to manage or cancel a subscription. Cancellation does not delete the bedtime calendar. Apple's system services process data under Apple's privacy policy: Apple Privacy Policy

Legacy trial records

Earlier versions recorded a local trial's start, deadline, expiration state and time-validation information, with an anchor in this device's system Keychain and a local App Group copy. This version does not require starting a trial and no longer uses those records to restrict free reminders or shields. They do not unlock paid skins.

The legacy Keychain record is not synchronized to a developer server, linked to a developer account or used as cross-device trial eligibility. It may remain in the system Keychain after deleting the app. The app currently has no separate control to delete this legacy Keychain record, and reinstalling is not guaranteed to clear it.

Local diagnostics and unused data services

To check scheduling, permission changes, snooze performance and purchase status, the app and extensions store operational events and recent state snapshots locally. They contain times, configuration states, permission results, selection counts, schedule identifiers, timing measurements and the necessary subscription/legacy-trial state described above. Performance events also use system logging. Operational events do not contain selected-app names, app content or selection tokens themselves.

The app has no advertising or third-party analytics SDK, uses no advertising tracking identifier, and connects to no developer upload endpoint or cloud sync. It does not automatically send these records to the developer. It requests no Health, camera, microphone, contacts or location access. Apple's operating-system diagnostics, App Store, system backups and external services you choose to use are governed by their respective system settings and policies.

Storage and retention

Reminder settings, app scope, the calendar, display snapshots, preferences and access caches are stored in the app's local container or an App Group used only by this app and its extensions. The legacy trial anchor is in this device's Keychain. Files supporting protection attributes use system file protection. Device backups may include some app data depending on your backup settings; the app provides no developer-operated cloud copy.

Calendar entries and preferences are not automatically deleted after a fixed number of days. They remain until you change them, delete individual entries or delete the app. Diagnostic snapshots are overwritten as the app runs. The event log rotates by size (about 128 KB, retaining about 96 KB of newer events when compacted), rather than a fixed day limit. Legacy trial and transaction-access caches may remain for compatibility or verification; their presence does not mean access is still valid. Apple controls retention of its transactions, system logs and backups under its policies and your settings.

Changes, deletion and requests

Open a date in the bedtime calendar to edit or delete that night's entry. Display snapshots are updated on a later refresh; the system controls when widgets visibly refresh. You can change reminder scope, language and skin in Settings. Turning reminders off stops this app's later schedules and removes its shields, without deleting the historical calendar or all diagnostics. The app currently has no one-tap control to erase all local data.

To remove ordinary app data, choose Delete App in system Settings → General → iPhone/iPad Storage → Bedtime Reminder. Offload App may retain documents and data. Deleting the app does not cancel an Apple subscription and is not guaranteed to remove legacy Keychain items, system backups or Apple's transaction records. The developer cannot remotely access or erase local data on your device. If you have emailed the developer, contact levinghost@outlook.com to request handling or deletion of email held by the developer. You need not include app-usage records, Apple passwords or payment details in a request.

Withdrawing permissions and stopping use

Screen Time: turn off Bedtime Reminder under system Settings → Screen Time → Apps with Screen Time Access (menu names vary by system version). The system revokes the relevant access and shields stop. When the app next observes revocation, it pauses the daily reminder plan, cancels its schedules and notifications, and updates its status. Revocation does not automatically delete local settings or the calendar. The calendar, manual entries and skins remain available.

Notifications: turn off Allow Notifications under system Settings → Notifications → Bedtime Reminder, or use the app's notification-settings link to open the system page. This stops notification presentation without automatically revoking Screen Time or its authorized shields, or deleting local entries. You can also turn off daily reminders on the home screen to stop schedules and shields. Cancel a skin subscription through Apple's subscription management; cancellation does not automatically revoke these permissions.

Email, sharing and external pages

Contact the developer only opens your chosen mail app or copies the address. You must review and send the email yourself. It does not automatically attach local diagnostics or bedtime entries. Your sender address, message and any attachments are handled by the relevant email services and the developer's Outlook mailbox only to reply, investigate your feedback and handle requests, not for advertising or sale. Email is retained for as long as needed to handle the inquiry. You can request deletion; legally required retention and the mail provider's backup rules may still apply.

Sharing the app gives its store link to your chosen share destination. Rating the app opens Apple's page. Public support/privacy pages are hosted on GitHub Pages, whose service may process IP addresses and other standard web-request information when visited. External apps or services you choose process data under their own policies. The app does not automatically send them local calendar entries, scope tokens or transaction caches.
