适用范围与开发者

本政策适用于 iPhone 和 iPad 上的“就寝提醒”及其提醒扩展和小组件。开发者联系方式：levinghost@outlook.com。App 无需注册开发者账户。iCloud 同步默认关闭；你开启后，App 使用你当前登录的 iCloud 账户及 Apple CloudKit 私人数据库同步就寝记录、提醒时间、所选皮肤和语言偏好，无需开发者提供的用户数据服务器。同步能力本身不解锁付费日历、手工编辑或额外皮肤。 每日提醒、屏幕使用时间遮罩、十分钟延后与提醒类小组件免费使用，无需试用或订阅。有效的 Apple 月订阅解锁就寝日历查看、手工编辑、记录类小组件及四款额外 App 与小组件皮肤；皇家紫始终免费。购买及使用这些付费功能不要求授予屏幕使用时间或通知权限。

提醒设置与本地通知

你设置的提醒时间、开关、范围模式、延后与解除状态、排程标识和必要时间信息保存在设备上，用于安排或取消提醒、处理“10 分钟后再提醒”与避免重复操作。允许通知后，App 通过 Apple 系统在本机安排通知；没有开发者远程推送提醒服务。开启 iCloud 同步时，提醒时间可以同步，每台设备仍分别授权并生成自己的排程。提醒开关、应用选择、通知请求、延后和已确认状态留在各设备；接收同步不会替另一台设备开启提醒或授权。Apple 的 CloudKit 可能使用系统变更通知唤起数据同步，这不代替本地提醒通知。 App 读取系统返回的通知授权和显示选项，用于显示权限状态、恢复排程和排查问题。通知是否在锁屏、横幅或通知中心出现，取决于你的系统设置、专注模式与系统调度。

屏幕使用时间与应用范围

屏幕使用时间访问由你在 Apple 的系统授权界面决定。App 保存“所有类别”范围模式，或你通过系统选择器选定的应用、类别和网站的不透明系统标识，用于在受支持范围显示和解除就寝遮罩。主 App 与提醒扩展通过设备上的 App Group 共享这些必要设置。屏幕使用时间授权、应用/类别/网站选择标识、使用数据及遮罩执行状态不进入 iCloud 同步；各设备须按系统流程分别授权和选择范围。 App 不读取所选应用里的内容、网页浏览内容、完整使用历史或全设备最后操作时间。它不以屏幕使用时间推断你是否睡着。现版本开启每日提醒计划需要屏幕使用时间授权；拒绝或撤销此权限会影响每日提醒与遮罩计划。就寝日历与皮肤的订阅资格独立判断，不要求此权限；日历记录也不是屏幕使用时间使用报告。

就寝记录与小组件

就寝记录来自你在免费提醒中点击“我准备睡觉了”或在有效订阅时手动录入，包括准备就寝时间、当晚目标、夜晚日期、时区、记录时间和来源。免费确认仍会在本机保存记录；未订阅时不开放日历查看、手工新增或修改。达标或晚睡状态仅比较记录时间与目标，不是睡眠检测或医学测量。没有回应提醒不会自动生成记录。凌晨 00:00–05:59 的目标归前一晚。开启 iCloud 同步后，上述就寝记录及其原夜晚日期、时区、绝对就寝时间和目标时间会同步到当前 iCloud 账户；换机或跨时区接收不重新计算历史夜晚归属。同步同时使用变更版本和删除标记合并离线修改，并可能暂存并发编辑的冲突版本，由你选择保留的记录。删除与全部清除会移除被删除的记录正文，保留阻止旧副本恢复所需的标识和因果版本元数据；不会提供被删除记录的隐藏恢复副本。 日历与记录类小组件在有效订阅时展示这些记录及其本地汇总；到期或退款后停止展示和手工编辑，不删除既有记录，重新验证有效订阅后恢复访问。主 App 向 App Group 写入显示快照，并为记录类小组件单独保存有限的、已验证权益时间投影；此投影不属于外观偏好，不能创建或延长订阅，不含 Apple 账户、支付资料、应用选择标识或完整交易记录。提醒类小组件不受此权益限制。有效订阅时可编辑或删除单晚记录；修改提醒时间或对同晚选择十分钟延后仍按免费提醒规则清除受影响的当前晚记录，已结束的历史夜晚保留。

语言与皮肤偏好

语言选择与皮肤偏好保存在设备的独立文件中。开启 iCloud 同步时，仅同步语言选项和所选皮肤标识；“跟随系统”仍按各设备系统语言解析。同步不会复制皮肤权益的验证时间或截止时间，付费皮肤由每台设备重新根据 Apple 验证的权益判断。语言供主 App、提醒扩展和小组件显示；所选皮肤及额外皮肤权益的已验证起止时间供主 App 与小组件显示。小组件只读取必要的外观权益投影，不读取 Apple 账户、支付资料或完整交易记录。系统屏幕使用时间遮罩使用固定的免费外观，不读取或使用付费皮肤权益。 皇家紫始终免费。其他四款皮肤由有效 Apple 月订阅解锁；订阅失效或退款后恢复皇家紫，并可保留你原先选择以便权益恢复后使用。修改语言或皮肤不会修改你的日历记录或提醒时间。

Apple 购买与交易信息

只有你另行确认购买，Apple 才会处理月订阅。Apple 负责 Apple 账户认证、支付、续费、取消和其交易记录的保留。App 不接收 Apple 账户密码、银行卡资料或完整账户资料。经 StoreKit 验证的商品与交易标识、交易环境、有效期、撤销和宽限期状态等必要信息在本机缓存，用于验证、恢复和显示日历与皮肤权益，不决定免费提醒或屏幕使用时间功能是否可用。交易缓存、验证投影、旧试用锚点和开发者自用资格不进入 iCloud 同步。同步历史或皮肤选择不创建、延长或恢复订阅，付费访问仍以本机 StoreKit 验证结果为准。 商品价格、币种和商店地区等系统返回信息用于显示本地价格和购买状态，也可能包含在本地诊断快照中。取消或管理订阅可从 App 的订阅页面进入 Apple 系统流程；取消不会删除就寝日历。Apple 的系统服务另按 Apple 的隐私政策处理数据：https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/。

旧版本试用记录

旧版本曾在本机记录试用起始、截止、到期状态及时间核验信息，并在这台设备的系统钥匙串保存试用锚点，在 App Group 保存本地副本。现版本不要求开始试用，也不再用这些旧记录限制免费提醒或遮罩；它们不会解锁付费日历或皮肤。 旧钥匙串记录不进入 App 的 iCloud 同步，也未同步到开发者服务器，不绑定开发者账户，也不作为跨设备试用资格。该记录可能在删除 App 后继续留在系统钥匙串；App 当前没有单独删除此旧钥匙串记录的入口，不能保证重装会清除它。

本地运行记录与未使用的数据服务

为核对排程、权限变化、延后性能和购买状态，App 与扩展在本机保存运行事件及最近状态快照。内容包括时间、配置状态、权限结果、选择数量、排程标识、耗时和前述必要订阅/旧试用状态；性能日志也使用系统日志。运行事件不记录所选应用名称、应用内容或选择标识本身。 App 没有广告或第三方分析 SDK，不使用广告跟踪标识，不接入开发者的上传接口，不自动向开发者传送这些资料。本地运行事件、权限快照和完整交易诊断不进入 iCloud 同步。为同步保存的账户隔离标识、设备副本标识、变更版本、待同步状态、最后成功时间及错误状态用于隔离账户、重试与排查，不包含 Apple 密码或支付资料。App 不请求健康、相机、麦克风、通讯录或定位权限。Apple 的操作系统诊断、App Store、系统备份以及你主动使用的外部服务，分别由相应系统设置和服务政策管理。

保存位置与期限

提醒设置、应用范围、日历、显示快照、偏好及权益缓存保存在 App 的本地容器或仅供本 App 及扩展使用的 App Group；旧试用锚点保存在设备钥匙串。支持保护属性的文件使用系统文件保护。系统备份可能包含部分 App 数据，取决于设备备份设置；App 不提供开发者托管的数据服务器。iCloud 同步关闭时仅使用本机资料；开启后，Apple CloudKit 私人数据库保存所选同步数据，占用你的 iCloud 空间，受账户、可用空间、网络和 Apple 服务条件影响。同步不保证即时完成，离线变更会保存在本机待同步状态中。 App 为不同 iCloud 账户分开保存本机同步工作区。退出或切换账户会暂停同步；换到另一账户后，需要重新选择仅恢复该账户云端数据或明确合并本机资料，旧账户资料不会自动上传到新账户。先前账户的本机缓存可能保留供切回处理。 日历和偏好不按固定天数自动删除，保留到你修改、删除相应或全部就寝记录，或删除 App。诊断状态快照随运行覆盖更新；事件日志按大小轮替（约 128 KB 上限，压缩时保留较新的约 96 KB），没有固定的按天保留期限。旧试用与交易权益缓存可为兼容或核验保留，不代表持续有效权益。Apple 保存的交易、系统日志和备份期限由 Apple 及你的设置决定。

修改、删除与请求处理

撤销权限与停止使用

Scope and developer

This policy covers Bedtime Reminder on iPhone and iPad, its reminder extensions and widgets. Contact the developer at levinghost@outlook.com. The app requires no developer account. iCloud sync is off by default. When you enable it, the app uses your signed-in iCloud account and Apple CloudKit private database to sync bedtime entries, reminder time, selected skin and language preference. No developer-operated user-data server is required. Sync itself does not unlock the paid calendar, manual editing or additional skins. Daily reminders, Screen Time shields, a 10-minute snooze and reminder widgets are free, without starting a trial or subscribing. An active Apple monthly subscription unlocks bedtime calendar viewing, manual editing, record-based widgets and four additional app and widget skins. Royal Purple is always free. Purchasing and using these paid features requires neither Screen Time nor notification permission.

Reminder settings and local notifications

Your reminder time, switches, scope mode, snooze and dismissal state, schedule identifiers and necessary timing information are stored on the device to schedule or cancel reminders, handle a 10-minute snooze and prevent duplicate actions. With notification permission, the app schedules local notifications through Apple's system. There is no developer-operated remote reminder push service. With iCloud sync enabled, reminder time can sync, while each device separately authorizes and creates its own schedule. Reminder switches, app selections, notification requests, snooze and acknowledgement state stay on each device. Receiving a sync does not enable reminders or grant permissions on another device. CloudKit may use Apple system change notifications to wake data synchronization; these do not replace local reminder notifications. The app reads notification authorization and presentation settings returned by the system to show permission status, recover schedules and diagnose problems. Lock Screen, banner and Notification Center presentation depends on your settings, Focus and system scheduling.

Screen Time and app scope

You decide whether to grant Screen Time access in Apple's system authorization interface. The app stores the all-categories scope mode or opaque system tokens for apps, categories and websites you select through the system picker, to show and dismiss bedtime shields within the supported scope. The main app and reminder extensions share these necessary settings in an on-device App Group. Screen Time authorization, app/category/website selection tokens, usage data and shield execution state are excluded from iCloud sync. Each device must separately use the system authorization and scope-selection flows. The app does not read selected-app content, browsing content, a complete usage history or the device-wide last interaction time. It does not infer sleep from Screen Time. This version requires Screen Time authorization to enable the daily reminder plan. Declining or revoking access affects daily reminder and shield plans. Subscription access to the bedtime calendar and skins is checked independently and requires no such permission. Calendar entries are not Screen Time usage reports.

Bedtime entries and widgets

Bedtime entries come from tapping I'm ready for bed in a free reminder, or from manual input with an active subscription. They include your reported preparation-for-bed time, nightly goal, night date, time zone, recording time and source. Free confirmations still save entries locally; calendar viewing, manual additions and changes are unavailable without a subscription. On-time or late status only compares an entry with its goal; it is not sleep detection or a medical measurement. An unanswered reminder creates no entry. Goals from 00:00 through 05:59 belong to the previous night. With iCloud sync enabled, these entries and their original night dates, time zones, absolute bedtimes and goals sync to the current iCloud account. Receiving them on a new device or in a different time zone does not reinterpret historical night ownership. Sync also uses change versions and deletion markers to merge offline changes. Concurrent edit versions may be retained as visible conflicts for you to resolve. Deletion and clear-all remove deleted entry content, retaining only the identifiers and causal version metadata needed to prevent older copies from restoring it; they do not provide a hidden recovery copy of deleted entries. With an active subscription, the calendar and record-based widgets display these entries and local summaries. Expiration or refund stops viewing and manual editing without deleting existing entries; access returns when an active subscription is verified again. The main app writes an App Group display snapshot and a separate, limited projection of verified access dates for record-based widgets. This projection is independent of appearance preferences, cannot create or extend a subscription, and contains no Apple Account, payment information, app-selection tokens or complete transaction records. Reminder widgets do not require this access. With an active subscription, you can edit or delete individual nights. Changing the reminder time or snoozing for ten minutes for the same night still clears the affected current-night entry under the free reminder rules; completed past nights are kept.

Language and skin preferences

Language and skin preferences are saved in separate on-device files. With iCloud sync enabled, only the language option and selected skin identifier sync. Follow system still resolves against each device's system language. Sync does not copy verified skin-access start or expiration dates; each device independently checks Apple-verified access for paid skins. The main app, reminder extensions and widgets read the language preference. The main app and widgets use the selected skin and verified start/end dates for additional-skin access. Widgets read only the necessary appearance-access projection, not your Apple Account, payment information or complete transaction records. System Screen Time shields use a fixed, free appearance without reading or using paid skin access. Royal Purple is always free. The other four skins require an active Apple monthly subscription. When access expires or is refunded, the appearance returns to Royal Purple; your previous choice may be retained for restored access. Changing language or skin does not change calendar entries or your reminder time.

Apple purchases and transaction information

Apple handles a monthly subscription only after you separately confirm a purchase. Apple manages Apple Account authentication, payment, renewal, cancellation and retention of its transaction records. The app does not receive your Apple Account password, card details or full account information. Necessary StoreKit-verified product and transaction identifiers, transaction environment, expiration, revocation and grace-period status are cached locally to verify, restore and display calendar and skin access. They do not determine access to free reminders or Screen Time features. Transaction caches, access projections, legacy trial anchors and developer self-use access are excluded from iCloud sync. Syncing history or a skin selection does not create, extend or restore a subscription; paid access still requires the device's StoreKit verification. System-provided price, currency and storefront information is used to show local pricing and purchase status and may appear in local diagnostic snapshots. The subscription page opens Apple's system flow to manage or cancel a subscription. Cancellation does not delete the bedtime calendar. Apple's system services process data under Apple's privacy policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/.

Legacy trial records

Earlier versions recorded a local trial's start, deadline, expiration state and time-validation information, with an anchor in this device's system Keychain and a local App Group copy. This version does not require starting a trial and no longer uses those records to restrict free reminders or shields. They do not unlock the paid calendar or skins. The legacy Keychain record is excluded from the app's iCloud sync and is not synchronized to a developer server, linked to a developer account or used as cross-device trial eligibility. It may remain in the system Keychain after deleting the app. The app currently has no separate control to delete this legacy Keychain record, and reinstalling is not guaranteed to clear it.

Local diagnostics and unused data services

To check scheduling, permission changes, snooze performance and purchase status, the app and extensions store operational events and recent state snapshots locally. They contain times, configuration states, permission results, selection counts, schedule identifiers, timing measurements and the necessary subscription/legacy-trial state described above. Performance events also use system logging. Operational events do not contain selected-app names, app content or selection tokens themselves. The app has no advertising or third-party analytics SDK, uses no advertising tracking identifier, and connects to no developer upload endpoint. It does not automatically send these records to the developer. Local operational events, permission snapshots and complete transaction diagnostics are excluded from iCloud sync. Account-isolation identifiers, replica identifiers, change versions, pending-sync state, last successful sync time and error state support account separation, retries and diagnosis; they contain no Apple passwords or payment details. It requests no Health, camera, microphone, contacts or location access. Apple's operating-system diagnostics, App Store, system backups and external services you choose to use are governed by their respective system settings and policies.

Storage and retention

Reminder settings, app scope, the calendar, display snapshots, preferences and access caches are stored in the app's local container or an App Group used only by this app and its extensions. The legacy trial anchor is in this device's Keychain. Files supporting protection attributes use system file protection. Device backups may include some app data depending on your backup settings; the app provides no developer-operated data server. With iCloud sync off, the app uses on-device data only. When enabled, Apple CloudKit private storage holds the selected sync data, uses your iCloud space and depends on account availability, free space, network and Apple service conditions. Sync is not guaranteed to be immediate; offline changes remain in persistent local pending state. The app keeps separate local sync workspaces for different iCloud accounts. Signing out or switching accounts pauses sync. Adopting another account requires choosing cloud-only restore or explicitly merging local data; old-account data is not automatically uploaded to a new account. Previous-account local caches may remain for handling after switching back. Calendar entries and preferences are not automatically deleted after a fixed number of days. They remain until you change them, delete individual or all bedtime entries, or delete the app. Diagnostic snapshots are overwritten as the app runs. The event log rotates by size (about 128 KB, retaining about 96 KB of newer events when compacted), rather than a fixed day limit. Legacy trial and transaction-access caches may remain for compatibility or verification; their presence does not mean access is still valid. Apple controls retention of its transactions, system logs and backups under its policies and your settings.

Changes, deletion and requests

With an active subscription, open a date in the bedtime calendar to edit or delete that night's entry. Without a subscription, these calendar actions are locked, but expiration or refund does not delete existing entries. Display snapshots are updated on a later refresh; the system controls when widgets visibly refresh. You can change reminder scope, language and skin in Settings. Turning reminders off stops this app's later schedules and removes its shields, without deleting the historical calendar or all diagnostics. Without a subscription, the free Settings → Privacy → Delete all bedtime records control still lets you delete all bedtime entries and calendar display snapshots after a second confirmation. This does not change reminders, permissions, subscriptions, skins or legacy trial records. For the currently linked iCloud dataset, deletion operations propagate to other devices using that account when sync is available. Offline deletion stays pending, so other devices may not clear immediately. Disabling sync pauses transfers; enabling it again merges changes and deletions made during the pause. Disabling sync or deleting the app does not automatically delete CloudKit copies. Deletion applies to the current dataset and its linked account; previous-account local caches or cloud data must be handled after returning to that account. Sync versions and deletion markers may remain to prevent old data from returning; deleted entry content is not retained as a recovery copy. Apple service backups and retention follow Apple's policies. The app currently has no one-tap control to erase all local data. To remove ordinary app data, choose Delete App in system Settings → General → iPhone/iPad Storage → Bedtime Reminder. Offload App may retain documents and data. Deleting the app does not cancel an Apple subscription and is not guaranteed to remove legacy Keychain items, system backups or Apple's transaction records. The developer cannot remotely access or erase local data on your device. If you have emailed the developer, contact levinghost@outlook.com to request handling or deletion of email held by the developer. You need not include app-usage records, Apple passwords or payment details in a request.

Withdrawing permissions and stopping use

Screen Time: turn off Bedtime Reminder under system Settings → Screen Time → Apps with Screen Time Access (menu names vary by system version). The system revokes the relevant access and shields stop. When the app next observes revocation, it pauses the daily reminder plan, cancels its schedules and notifications, and updates its status. Revocation does not automatically delete local settings or the calendar. Paid access to the calendar and skins is checked independently and requires neither permission. iCloud: pause sync in the app's Settings → iCloud sync, or change this app's access in system iCloud settings. Pausing retains local entries and cloud copies; enabling it again resumes synchronization. Without iCloud sign-in, service availability or successful sync, local reminders and existing data remain usable. Notifications: turn off Allow Notifications under system Settings → Notifications → Bedtime Reminder, or use the app's notification-settings link to open the system page. This stops notification presentation without automatically revoking Screen Time or its authorized shields, or deleting local entries. You can also turn off daily reminders on the home screen to stop schedules and shields. Cancel the calendar and skins subscription through Apple's subscription management; cancellation does not automatically revoke these permissions.
