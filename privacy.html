ChipDial Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 9, 2026

ChipDial is an offline machining calculator. The app does not require an account and does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data.

Data stored on your device

Calculation history and favorites may be stored locally on your device so you can review or restore them. This information is not sent to the developer or any third party.

Tracking and third parties

ChipDial does not use advertising, analytics, tracking technologies, or third-party data collection services. The app does not require access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos, or health data.

Children

ChipDial does not knowingly collect information from children or any other users.

Changes and contact

If you have privacy questions, email levinghost@outlook.com. If the app’s data practices change, this policy will be updated before those changes take effect.
